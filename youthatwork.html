About the Program

Helping young people work, earn, and grow

Youth@Work gives LA County’s future workforce first-time, paid work experiences in high-growth and high-road sectors, building skills that meet the region’s talent needs now and in the future.

LA County youth ages 14 to 24 get personal enrichment and hands-on paid work experience through America’s Job Centers of California (AJCC) and local employer partners.

Program Benefits

What Youth@Work offers

Paid work experience

160 hours of personal enrichment training and paid work experience for youth across LA County.

Career readiness support

Paid training, mentorship, and hands-on experience in high-demand industries.

Equity-centered opportunities

Support for youth affected by economic and climate crises and by systemic barriers, including foster, unhoused, LGBTQ+, and justice-involved youth.

Pathways into high-demand industries

Training aligned with regional workforce needs in healthcare, infrastructure, technology, and creative sectors.

Leadership and professional growth

Workshops on leadership, career planning, and peer support build confidence and lasting networks.

Partnerships with employers

Employers get access to an emerging talent pipeline for up to four months of part-time work, with wage and insurance subsidies.

For Employers

Youth@Work enrolls employers as host sites. Host sites work with an AJCC to enroll as a participating employer, identify employer demand and commitments, complete a host site agreement with a detailed training plan for the 140 hours, name a manager and point of contact for the lead AJCC, and validate hours worked by youth.

DEO is looking for employers in the region’s high-growth sectors that offer pathways to hire: Aerospace and Defense, Life Sciences, Construction and Infrastructure, Creative Sector, Early Care and Education, Healthcare, Hospitality and Tourism, Information Technology and Trade, and Transportation and Logistics.

Eligibility for businesses

  • Located in LA County
  • Must meet with an AJCC to enroll as a participating employer
  • Complete a host site agreement with a detailed training plan
  • Provide managerial oversight and a point of contact
  • Validate hours worked by participants

How to partner

Employers across LA County can become a host site by emailing hireayouth@opportunity.lacounty.gov. AJCC staff will reach out within 48 hours.

Become a host site

For Youth

  • Personal enrichment programming through DEO’s Transition Age Youth – World of Work class with an AJCC, including life skills, career pathway planning, and job portfolio development and readiness
  • Traditional, year-round Youth@Work Program:
    • 140 hours of paid work experience with hundreds of participating employer partners as host sites
  • Post-program connection to additional hiring, training, and education opportunities
  • Connection to employment in high-growth sectors
  • Connection to supportive services and a mentorship program

Eligibility for youth

  • LA County resident
  • Ages 14 to 24 at time of enrollment
  • Legal right to work in the U.S.
  • High-need communities
  • Current or former foster youth
  • Impacted by the justice system
  • Currently or previously experiencing homelessness
  • Identify as LGBTQ+

Get started

Interested and eligible youth can sign up with the interest form. AJCC staff will reach out within 48 hours.
